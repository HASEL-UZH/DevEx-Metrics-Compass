  • Adoption Rate

    Category: Process🏆 Top 5% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Relates to engineers’ uptake and sustained use of internal tools, workflows, and platforms, reflecting how effectively these resources integrate into daily workflows and solve critical problems. It serves as a key metric for assessing DevEx success and alignment with productivity goals.

  • Cycle Time

    Category: Process🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the time from code commit to code running in production. Shows how quickly team can respond to business needs.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneCircleCIGoogleGrammarlyLloyds Banking GroupMicrosoftMicrosoftPostmanSlackStripeVanguardXero
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Murphy et al. (2019)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Sadowski et al. (2019)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Deployment Frequency

    Category: Customer🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    How often an organization deploys code/builds to a production environment, regardless of whether that change is yet visible to end users. Higher deployment frequency indicates a more agile and responsive delivery process.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonGitLabGlassdoorGoogleMonzoNetflixPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DORA Framework📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Hatica (2024)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Solanke (2024)

  • Deployments per Developer

    Category: Output📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Quantifies the amount of weekly deployments per developer.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Employee Retention Rate

    Category: Business📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    How well an organization retains its employees over a given period.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄McKinsey Framework

  • Perceived Productivity

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    The self-reported productivity by an individual (might be aggregated on team level).

    Other companies applying the metric: GitHubGoogleGoogleIntercomPostman

  • Throughput

    Category: Output🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Quantifies the number of features, stories, PRs / diffs, … completed per developer within a time interval (e.g. week).

  • Time to Nth Pull Request

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the time it takes for a new engineer to create their Nth pull request (PR) after joining a development team. For example, the time to 1st and 10th PR at Spotify.
