3 Developer Experience Metrics in the DevEx Framework

The DevEx Framework is referenced by 3 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice. See the DevEx source.

Open these DevEx metrics in the interactive Compass →

  • Cognitive Load Score

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    Self-reported mental effort required to perform tasks. Avoiding multitasking, high WIP (work in progress) counts, and attention splitting to maintain focus and reduce cognitive overhead.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Chen (2026)📄Forsgren et al. (2024)📄Gonçales et al. (2019)📄Kanat-Alexander (2025)📄Solanke (2024)

  • Feedback Latency

    Category: Team🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    The average time between a developer action (e.g., code push, PR submission, build trigger) and the developer receiving a response (e.g., CI/test results, build status, review comments).

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Forsgren et al. (2024)

  • Focus Time

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The ability to work in focus or flow, i.e. uninterrupted, in meeting-free blocks that allow the developer to concentrate deeply on their work. Often reported as percentage of total time or average time.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGitHubGoodRxGoogleMicrosoftStripeUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Brown et al. (2023)📄Forsgren et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework
