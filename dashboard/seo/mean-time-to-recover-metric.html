Mean Time to Recover

Mean Time to Recover (MTTR) is a key DevOps and IT reliability metric that measures the average time it takes to restore a system after a failure. It reflects how quickly teams can diagnose, fix, and recover from incidents. Time from bug report to fix deployment. Shows quality response capability.
