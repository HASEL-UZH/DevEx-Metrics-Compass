  • Time Loss

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Quantifies the percentage (or hours) of developer time lost each week to obstacles in the work environment (e.g., unclear requirements, excessive meetings, context switching, or technical debt) typically collected via self-reported survey. Often expressed as a cost (translatable into engineering-dollar terms) to make the impact tangible to business stakeholders.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoodRx
