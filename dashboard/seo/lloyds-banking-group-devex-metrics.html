  • Branch Lifetime

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The time elapsed between branch creation until merge to main/production.

    Other companies applying the metric: Stripe
    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Code Reviewer Response Time

    Category: Team📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures how long it takes, in business hours, for code reviewers to respond to each update of the code review from the author.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGoogleLinkedInMeta
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄DX (2024b)📄Gitpod (2025)📄gun.io (2025)📄LinkedIn DPH Framework📄McKinsey Framework

  • Code Reviews per Developer

    Category: Output📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Quantifies the number of weekly code reviews per developer.

    Other companies applying the metric: Uber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Cycle Time

    Category: Process🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the time from code commit to code running in production. Shows how quickly team can respond to business needs.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneCircleCIGoogleGrammarlyMicrosoftMicrosoftPostmanSlackSpotifyStripeVanguardXero
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Murphy et al. (2019)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Sadowski et al. (2019)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Developer Friction Score

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    A self-reported rating or open-text response of the obstacles, delays, or friction developers encounter in their daily lives, e.g. from accessing tools, applicatiosn or completing workflow tasks.

    Other companies applying the metric: eBayGoogle
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Microsoft EngThrive Framework

  • Post-Submission Edit Ratio

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    The percentage of code changes within a pull request that occur after it has been submitted for review. Aims to minimize back-and-forth during review, enabling quicker, more decisive reviews and faster integration.

    Other companies applying the metric: Google
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Cheng et al. (2022)📄Murphy et al. (2019)

  • Pull Request Cycle Time

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures the total time a pull request spends in all stages of the development pipeline, from the first commit to when the pull request is merged into the codebase (e.g. to the main/production branch). It provides insights into the efficiency of the code review and merging process, helping teams identify bottlenecks and improve workflows.

    Other companies applying the metric: Atlassian
    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Microsoft EngThrive Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Pull Request Size

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    An indicator of overall code review efficiency. Small PRs mean faster pickup time, reviews, and cycles–translating to faster time to value. Larger PRs often result in fewer meaningful comments. For example, PRs with more than 250 lines of code could be tagged.

    Other companies applying the metric: MetaMicrosoftPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton

  • Technical Debt Ratio

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Is an estimate of maintenance burden in codebase. It essentially compares the cost of fixing existing technical debt to the total cost of developing the software.

    Other companies applying the metric: SonarSourceSquare
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Besker et al. (2019)📄Gitpod (2025)

  • Time spent Coding

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The amount or proportion of a developer's working time actively spent writing or editing code, as measured by IDE telemetry. Excludes building, testing, meetings, and other non-coding activities. Helps to identify whether developers have sufficient uninterrupted time for core development work.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoogleMetaMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Meyer (2021)

  • Time to Nth Pull Request

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the time it takes for a new engineer to create their Nth pull request (PR) after joining a development team. For example, the time to 1st and 10th PR at Spotify.

    Other companies applying the metric: PelotonSpotifySquareStripe
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Peloton
