5 Developer Experience Metrics in the LinkedIn DPH Framework

The LinkedIn DPH Framework is referenced by 5 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice. See the LinkedIn DPH source.

  • CI Build Time

    Category: Process🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Refers to the (median or mean) duration of successful builds in a continuous integration (CI) pipeline. Might be quantified for individual or a group of commits.

    Other companies applying the metric: LatticeLinkedInLinkedIn
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • CI Determinism

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    The opposite of test flakiness: the chance that a test suite’s result will be valid (not a flake) ensures that identical inputs in a continuous integration (CI) pipeline always produce the same outputs (binaries, artifacts, test results). This guarantees reproducibility, reliability, and traceability in software builds, critical for debugging, compliance, and safety-critical systems.

    Other companies applying the metric: LinkedIn

  • CI Pipeline Failure Rate

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of builds or automated workflows in a continuous integration (CI) system that fail due to errors (e.g., compilation issues, test failures, dependency conflicts).

    Other companies applying the metric: LatticeSlack
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Code Reviewer Response Time

    Category: Team📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures how long it takes, in business hours, for code reviewers to respond to each update of the code review from the author.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGoogleLinkedInLloyds Banking GroupMeta
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄DX (2024b)📄Gitpod (2025)📄gun.io (2025)📄McKinsey Framework

  • Deployment Success Rate

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The percentage of deployment (CI/CD) runs that complete successfully.

    Other companies applying the metric: LinkedIn
    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Peng et al. (2023)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)
