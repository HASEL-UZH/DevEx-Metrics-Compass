  • Adoption Rate

    Category: Process🏆 Top 5% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Relates to engineers’ uptake and sustained use of internal tools, workflows, and platforms, reflecting how effectively these resources integrate into daily workflows and solve critical problems. It serves as a key metric for assessing DevEx success and alignment with productivity goals.

  • Ease of Delivery

    Category: Developer Enablement🏅 Top 10% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    Refers to how simple, straightforward, and frictionless it is for developers or teams to deliver software, features, or updates through their development processes and tooling. Captures the perceived and actual effort required to move work from development to production.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmplitudeChimeGoogleIntercomLatticePostman

  • Engineer Engagement

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Refers to the emotional commitment, motivation, and involvement that software engineers or developers have toward their work, team, and organization.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmplitudeIntercomPostman
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Jetbrains (2024)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Focus Time

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The ability to work in focus or flow, i.e. uninterrupted, in meeting-free blocks that allow the developer to concentrate deeply on their work. Often reported as percentage of total time or average time.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGitHubGoogleMicrosoftStripeUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Brown et al. (2023)📄DevEx Framework📄Forsgren et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Service Uptime Percentage

    Category: System⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of time services and apps are available/operational, and the rate of production incidents (outages, change failures, time-to-recovery): the system's track record of staying up and recovering quickly. An improved rate reduces time teams spend addressing bugs and outages, increasing operational efficiency.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonDoordashMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Speed

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Relates to how quickly software engineers can accomplish their tasks, focusing on the efficiency of workflows and processes that enable rapid delivery without compromising quality. This includes quantiative metrics (logs for task durations, deployment statistics, and CI/CD pipeline metrics) and qualitative metrics (developer surveys, diary studies, and interviews to understand perceptions and identify pain points).

    Other companies applying the metric: Google
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024a)📄D’Angelo et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Microsoft EngThrive Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Time Loss

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Quantifies the percentage (or hours) of developer time lost each week to obstacles in the work environment (e.g., unclear requirements, excessive meetings, context switching, or technical debt) typically collected via self-reported survey. Often expressed as a cost (translatable into engineering-dollar terms) to make the impact tangible to business stakeholders.

    Other companies applying the metric: Postman
