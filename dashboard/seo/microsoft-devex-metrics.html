  • Burnout Risk Score

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Evaluates developers’ mental health, job fulfillment, and workplace conditions. It serves as a critical indicator of systemic issues that harm productivity and retention.

  • Change Failure Rate

    Category: Quality🏆 Top 5% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    This metric measures the percentage of deployments that cause failures in production, requiring hotfixes or rollbacks. A lower change failure rate indicates a more reliable delivery process.

  • Discoverability of Documentation and Expertise

    Category: Process🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Refers to how easily developers can locate critical information (e.g., codebase guides, API references) or identify colleagues with specific knowledge.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Experience of New Members

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Is a combination of satisfaction, well-being and integration indicators to ensure new developers feel supported, included and able to contribute effectively.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Focus Time

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The ability to work in focus or flow, i.e. uninterrupted, in meeting-free blocks that allow the developer to concentrate deeply on their work. Often reported as percentage of total time or average time.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGitHubGoodRxGoogleStripeUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Brown et al. (2023)📄DevEx Framework📄Forsgren et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Impact

    Category: Developer Enablement📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Evaluates the tangible outcomes and value delivered by development efforts. Unlike raw outputs (e.g., lines of code), impact focuses on business results and user-centric effects of software work. Making significant contributions to one’s business unit or area of ownership, ensuring that work has a tangible impact.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Number of Handoffs

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures how many times work is transferred between individuals, teams, or systems during a workflow.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄McKinsey Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Operational Activity

    Category: System📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Refers to the routine tasks and processes required to maintain, monitor, and support software systems in production. These activities ensure the system remains stable, secure, and performant while minimizing downtime.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Pull Request Size

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    An indicator of overall code review efficiency. Small PRs mean faster pickup time, reviews, and cycles–translating to faster time to value. Larger PRs often result in fewer meaningful comments. For example, PRs with more than 250 lines of code could be tagged.

    Other companies applying the metric: Lloyds Banking GroupMetaPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton

  • Quality of Code Reviews

    Category: Team⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures quality of code reviews contributed by team members.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Service Uptime Percentage

    Category: System⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of time services and apps are available/operational, and the rate of production incidents (outages, change failures, time-to-recovery): the system's track record of staying up and recovering quickly. An improved rate reduces time teams spend addressing bugs and outages, increasing operational efficiency.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonDoordashGoodRx
    Research recommending the metric: 📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Time for Onboarding New Hires

    Category: Business🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the onboarding time for new members, also including HR-related onboarding.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Time spent Coding

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The amount or proportion of a developer's working time actively spent writing or editing code, as measured by IDE telemetry. Excludes building, testing, meetings, and other non-coding activities. Helps to identify whether developers have sufficient uninterrupted time for core development work.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoogleLloyds Banking GroupMeta
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Meyer (2021)

  • Time to Market for New Features

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the duration from the initial conception of a feature to its availability for end-users.

    Other companies applying the metric: UberUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄EEBO Framework📄Ko (2019)

  • Value-Added Time vs. Wait Time

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Per the SPACE framework, decomposes the time work spends in a system into value-added time (actively being worked on) and wait time (idle — in queues, blocked, or awaiting handoff), distinguishing productive flow from delay.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Velocity

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The amount of work completed per sprint/time period. Helps with planning and tracking.

    Other companies applying the metric: AtlassianSpotifyUberUber
