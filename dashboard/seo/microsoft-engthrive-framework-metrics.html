8 Developer Experience Metrics in the Microsoft EngThrive Framework

The Microsoft EngThrive Framework is referenced by 8 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice. See the Microsoft EngThrive source.

  • Bad Developer Days

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    A composite telemetry metric counting the share of developer-days affected by friction events such as context switching, build failures, incident response, or compliance overhead.

  • Burnout Risk Score

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Evaluates developers’ mental health, job fulfillment, and workplace conditions. It serves as a critical indicator of systemic issues that harm productivity and retention.

    Other companies applying the metric: Microsoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Custom Quality Metric

    Category: Quality🏅 Top 10% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    Relates to code reliability, maintainability, and alignment with user/business outcomes. It may be measured through metrics that assess both technical rigor and real-world impact, emphasizing sustainable practices over superficial outputs. Usually highly company-specific implementation.

    Other companies applying the metric: AdyenGitHubGoogleMicrosoft

  • Developer Friction Score

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    A self-reported rating or open-text response of the obstacles, delays, or friction developers encounter in their daily lives, e.g. from accessing tools, applicatiosn or completing workflow tasks.

    Other companies applying the metric: eBayGoogleLloyds Banking Group

  • Ease of Delivery

    Category: Developer Enablement🏅 Top 10% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    Refers to how simple, straightforward, and frictionless it is for developers or teams to deliver software, features, or updates through their development processes and tooling. Captures the perceived and actual effort required to move work from development to production.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmplitudeChimeGoodRxGoogleIntercomLatticePostman
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024a)📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄D’Angelo et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Pull Request Cycle Time

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures the total time a pull request spends in all stages of the development pipeline, from the first commit to when the pull request is merged into the codebase (e.g. to the main/production branch). It provides insights into the efficiency of the code review and merging process, helping teams identify bottlenecks and improve workflows.

    Other companies applying the metric: AtlassianLloyds Banking Group
    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Speed

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Relates to how quickly software engineers can accomplish their tasks, focusing on the efficiency of workflows and processes that enable rapid delivery without compromising quality. This includes quantiative metrics (logs for task durations, deployment statistics, and CI/CD pipeline metrics) and qualitative metrics (developer surveys, diary studies, and interviews to understand perceptions and identify pain points).

    Other companies applying the metric: GoodRxGoogle
