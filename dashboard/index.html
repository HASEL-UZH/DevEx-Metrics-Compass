Welcome to the Developer Experience Metrics Compass

Frameworks like SPACE, DORA, and others have introduced a broad range of developer experience metrics — from satisfaction and flow to deployment frequency and code churn. But with abundance comes a challenge: which metrics actually matter for your team, context, and goals?

The DevEx Metrics Compass curates metrics from both research and industry, making them explorable across multiple dimensions — automated vs. qualitative data collection, research or industry origin, outcome goals, company size, and GenAI relevance.

The goal is not to measure more, but to measure more deliberately. Choose how you'd like to start:
