Welcome to the Developer Experience Metrics Compass

Frameworks like SPACE, DORA, and others have introduced a broad range of developer experience metrics — from satisfaction and flow to deployment frequency and code churn. But with abundance comes a challenge: which metrics actually matter for your team, context, and goals?

The DevEx Metrics Compass curates metrics from both research and industry, making them explorable across multiple dimensions — automated vs. qualitative data collection, research or industry origin, outcome goals, company size, and GenAI relevance.

The goal is not to measure more, but to measure more deliberately. Choose how you'd like to start:

Maturity Assessment: Question 2

Is your team / company systematically collecting feedback to identify specific pain points and opportunities for improvements in the developer experience?

Your team's / company's suggested maturity level is "Level II - Managed"

Topic Description and Characteristics
Processes Basic, repeatable processes (e.g., basic issue tracking)
Survey Data Periodic
Not specific to DX
Analysis of Survey Data Basic analysis conducted to identify common themes/trends. Data stored in a centralized, accessible location. Mostly analyzed by HR or team leads.
Telemetry Data Continuous capture of basic telemetry/log-based metrics not tailored to DX-specific topics are collected.
DX-specific telemetry/log-based metrics are collected but not considered to assess the impact of DX initiatives.
Analysis of Telemetry Data Data analyzed monthly by team or division head. Data from two different periods is compared to identify general performance or common issues.
Development and Implementation of Initiatives Non-centrally coordinated bottom-up DX initiatives of teams and/or divisions.
Focus of Initiatives Effort-oriented metrics (e.g., lines of code)
Impact Assessment None.
Roles None.

Success Factors for Next Level:

  • Consistent application of developer experience surveys.
  • Investing in tools for automated collection of a few key quantiative metrics.
  • Establishing baselines.
  • Fostering psychological safety around data (e.g., not linking productivity metrics to salary).

Task: Explore what kind of more elaborate frameworks exist. An good way to start could be the SPACE (Satisfaction and well-being, Performance, Activity, Communication and collaboration, and Efficiency and flow) framework. Extend the use of surveys (qualitative metrics).

Maturity Assessment: Question 4

Is there an internal developer experience research team in place? Is your team / company a developer experience innovator?

Your team's / company's suggested maturity level is "Level IV - Quantitatively Managed"

Topic Description and Characteristics
Processes Focus on efficiency gains, reducing obstacles, and enhancing developer flow.
Survey Data Periodic
Granular surveys on specific DX topics (e.g., effectiveness of tools, time lost to unproductive work)
Analysis of Survey Data Statistically robust analysis of survey data conducted continuously by DX lead/manager. Advanced analytical techniques (e.g., sentiment analysis, predictive modeling) are employed to identify root causes and anticipate future issues. Insights are directly linked to performance metrics and strategic objectives.
Telemetry Data Sophisticated engineering intelligence platforms or in-house systems integrating data from across the entire development ecosystem throughout the software development life cycle (SDLC).
Analysis of Telemetry Data Continuously or on a daily basis. Data dashboards are real-time, and dedicated DX experts perform deep-dive statistical analysis and generate regular reports (e.g., daily/weekly performance summaries, ad-hoc deep dives on anomalies).
Development and Implementation of Initiatives Continuous monitoring of DX metrics and anomaly detection.
Strategic investments in developer tooling and infrastructure based on clear ROI.
Focus of Initiatives Outcome-oriented metrics (e.g., deployment frequency)
Impact Assessment Dedicated dashboards that show the impact of DX initiatives on business outcomes (e.g., ROI). Continuous experimentation and A/B testing of process changes.
Roles Dedicated role or team focusing on DX

Success Factors for Next Level:

  • Leveraging AI/ML for predictive insights.
  • External benchmarking and thought leadership.
  • Active contribution to the wider engineering community
  • Treating developer experience as a first-class product.

Task: See what competitors do, especially large companies. What are metrics that are used / collected a lot? Identify gaps in the metrics and develop new metrics.
All metrics used by enterprise-size companies (5000+ developers) are shown when you click on the "Show metrics" button.
