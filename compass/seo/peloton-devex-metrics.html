    Category: Quality🏆 Top 5% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

  • Line Coverage

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    Line Coverage is a code coverage metric that measures the percentage of lines in a codebase that have been executed during testing. It helps determine how well a test suite exercises the code.

    Other companies applying the metric: Glassdoor
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Mean Time to Recover

    Category: System🏅 Top 10% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Mean Time to Recover (MTTR) is a key DevOps and IT reliability metric that measures the average time it takes to restore a system after a failure. It reflects how quickly teams can diagnose, fix, and recover from incidents. Time from bug report to fix deployment. Shows quality response capability.

    Other companies applying the metric: AdobeDropboxeBayGlassdoorLinkedInMozilla
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Hatica (2024)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Srinivasan et al. (2025)

  • Pull Request Size

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    An indicator of overall code review efficiency. Small PRs mean faster pickup time, reviews, and cycles–translating to faster time to value. Larger PRs often result in fewer meaningful comments. For example, PRs with more than 250 lines of code could be tagged.

    Other companies applying the metric: Lloyds Banking GroupMetaMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton

  • Time to Nth Pull Request

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the time it takes for a new engineer to create their Nth pull request (PR) after joining a development team. For example, the time to 1st and 10th PR at Spotify.
