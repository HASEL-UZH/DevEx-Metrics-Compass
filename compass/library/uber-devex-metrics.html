10 Developer Experience Metrics used at Uber

Uber is associated with 10 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice.

  • Code Reviews per Developer

    Category: Output📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Quantifies the number of weekly code reviews per developer.

    Other companies applying the metric: Lloyds Banking Group
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Design Docs per Developer

    Category: Output📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Quantifies the weekly number of design documents generated per engineer.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Focus Time

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The ability to work in focus or flow, i.e. uninterrupted, in meeting-free blocks that allow the developer to concentrate deeply on their work. Often reported as percentage of total time or average time.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGitHubGoodRxGoogleMicrosoftStripe
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Brown et al. (2023)📄DevEx Framework📄Forsgren et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Local Build Time

    Category: System🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the time, in seconds, developers spend waiting for their builds to finish locally during development. Impacts developer flow and productivity. Considered e.g. as median (P50) or P90.

    Other companies applying the metric: LatticeLinkedInTwitter/X
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Number of Escaped Bugs

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the number of defects or issues that escape testing and reach production. It measures the effectiveness of testing processes and helps identify weaknesses in quality assurance.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Sadowski et al. (2019)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Treude et al. (2015)

  • Produced Code

    Category: Output🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Tracks contributions to the codebase, for example by number of lines of code.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoogleGoogleMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Caulo et al. (2021)📄Cheng et al. (2022)📄Duncan (1988)📄Edberg and Bowman (2015)📄Gitpod (2025)📄Gousios et al. (2008)📄Green et al. (2023)📄Ko (2019)📄Kuutila et al. (2020)📄Lima et al. (2015)📄Madeyski and Szała (2007) 📄Moser et al. (2008)📄Oliveira et al. (2017)📄Oliveira et al. (2020)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Sarimbekov et al (2014📄SPACE Framework📄Unluturk and Kurtel (2016)📄Wagner and Deissenboeck (2019)

  • Task Completion Rate

    Category: Output🏅 Top 10% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the rate of work completion — the volume of task units (e.g., story points, function points, requirements) completed per individual or team, typically tracked per sprint or time period, and optionally expressed as a percentage of planned work completed.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Chen (2026)📄Edberg and Bowman (2015)📄Guglielmi et al. (2025)📄Ko (2019)📄McKinsey Framework📄Murphy-Hill and Wagner (2019)📄Oliveira et al. (2017)📄Parrish et al. (2004)📄Sadowski et al. (2019)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Vogelezang and Heeringen (2019)📄Wagner and Deissenboeck (2019)

  • Throughput

    Category: Output🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Quantifies the number of features, stories, PRs / diffs, … completed per developer within a time interval (e.g. week).

    Other companies applying the metric: AdyenAtlassianAtlassianBooking.comBooking.comCarGurusCircleCIDropboxGitHubMonzoSpotifyStripeToastWebflowXero
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Brumby et al. (2019)📄Caulo et al. (2021)📄DX (2025)📄gun.io (2025)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄Kuutila et al. (2020)📄Mockus (2009)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Treude and Filho (2019)

  • Time to Market for New Features

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the duration from the initial conception of a feature to its availability for end-users.

    Other companies applying the metric: Microsoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄EEBO Framework📄Ko (2019)

  • Velocity

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The amount of work completed per sprint/time period. Helps with planning and tracking.

    Other companies applying the metric: AtlassianMicrosoftSpotify
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Gitpod (2025)📄James (2024)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)
