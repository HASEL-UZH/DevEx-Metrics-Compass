6 Developer Experience Metrics used at Meta

Meta is associated with 6 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice.

  • Code Reviewer Response Time

    Category: Team📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures how long it takes, in business hours, for code reviewers to respond to each update of the code review from the author.

    Other companies applying the metric: CapitalOneGoogleLinkedInLloyds Banking Group
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄DX (2024b)📄Gitpod (2025)📄gun.io (2025)📄LinkedIn DPH Framework📄McKinsey Framework

  • Diff Authoring Time

    Category: Process🏅 Top 10% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    Aims to capture all active human developer time spent on a particular diff, split by the different roles involved. This information is made available in a privacy-aware manner. Active human work on a computer tends to be highly sequential, even if such individual sequences can be extremely short.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoogleGoogleGrammarly
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Beller et al. (2020)📄Beller et al. (2025)📄Caulo et al. (2021)📄Cheng et al. (2022)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Jaspan and Sadowski (2019)📄Kokrehel and Bilicki (2021)📄Madeyski and Szała (2007) 📄Oliveira et al. (2020)📄Peng et al. (2023)📄Solanke (2024)

  • Diff Risk Score

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Predicts the likelihood of a code change (diff) causing a production incident.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Mockus (2025)

  • Pull Request Size

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    An indicator of overall code review efficiency. Small PRs mean faster pickup time, reviews, and cycles–translating to faster time to value. Larger PRs often result in fewer meaningful comments. For example, PRs with more than 250 lines of code could be tagged.

    Other companies applying the metric: Lloyds Banking GroupMicrosoftPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton

  • Release Frequency

    Category: Customer📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    How often features reach users (independent of deployment frequency, such as when feature flags or staged rollouts are used).

    Other companies applying the metric: Netflix
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Gitpod (2025)

  • Time spent Coding

    Category: Developer Enablement🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The amount or proportion of a developer's working time actively spent writing or editing code, as measured by IDE telemetry. Excludes building, testing, meetings, and other non-coding activities. Helps to identify whether developers have sufficient uninterrupted time for core development work.

    Other companies applying the metric: GoogleLloyds Banking GroupMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Meyer (2021)
