  • Application Startup Duration

    Category: System🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The time needed to startup an application locally.

  • Change Failure Rate

    Category: Quality🏆 Top 5% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    This metric measures the percentage of deployments that cause failures in production, requiring hotfixes or rollbacks. A lower change failure rate indicates a more reliable delivery process.

    Other companies applying the metric: AdyenAmplitudeBooking.comCircleCIDropboxEtsyGitHubGlassdoorMicrosoftMonzoPelotonToastWebflow
    Research recommending the metric: 📄APEX framework📄DORA Framework📄DX (2024b)📄DX (2025)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄gun.io (2025)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • CI Build Time

    Category: Process🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Refers to the (median or mean) duration of successful builds in a continuous integration (CI) pipeline. Might be quantified for individual or a group of commits.

    Other companies applying the metric: LinkedInLinkedIn
    Research recommending the metric: 📄LinkedIn DPH Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • CI Pipeline Failure Rate

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of builds or automated workflows in a continuous integration (CI) system that fail due to errors (e.g., compilation issues, test failures, dependency conflicts).

    Other companies applying the metric: Slack
    Research recommending the metric: 📄LinkedIn DPH Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Ease of Delivery

    Category: Developer Enablement🏅 Top 10% most-referenced🧑‍💻 Developer Experience🎯 AI Impact

    Refers to how simple, straightforward, and frictionless it is for developers or teams to deliver software, features, or updates through their development processes and tooling. Captures the perceived and actual effort required to move work from development to production.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmplitudeChimeGoodRxGoogleIntercomPostman
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024a)📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄D’Angelo et al. (2024)📄gun.io (2025)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄Microsoft EngThrive Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Linting Duration

    Category: System🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    The duration needed to lint apps and packages locally or in a pipeline.

  • Local Build Time

    Category: System🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the time, in seconds, developers spend waiting for their builds to finish locally during development. Impacts developer flow and productivity. Considered e.g. as median (P50) or P90.

    Other companies applying the metric: LinkedInTwitter/XUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄SPACE Framework

  • Number of Deployments

    Category: Customer📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures the number of deployments.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Bao et al. (2022)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Test Runner Duration

    Category: System🧑‍💻 Developer Experience

    Measures the duration to run tests for various apps and packages locally.
