6 Developer Experience Metrics used at Amazon

  • Change Lead Time

    Category: Process🏅 Top 10% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    This metric measures the time it takes for a code commit or change to be successfully deployed to production. It reflects the efficiency of your software delivery process to deliver value to customers.

  • Deployment Frequency

    Category: Customer🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    How often an organization deploys code/builds to a production environment, regardless of whether that change is yet visible to end users. Higher deployment frequency indicates a more agile and responsive delivery process.

  • Reliability Score

    Category: System⭐ Product Excellence

    A composite reliability measure covering availability (uptime %), latency (response time percentiles), performance, and scalability (behavior under load). DORA's original 2021 methodology measured this via self-rated survey ('are you meeting your reliability targets?'), but the underlying components can also be tracked quantitatively via system telemetry.

  • Service Readiness Score

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    A composite score assessing how well individual services meet operational standards (e.g., monitoring/alarm coverage, runbook completeness, on-call readiness, error-budget adherence) used to track production maturity across a microservices portfolio.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)

  • Service Uptime Percentage

    Category: System⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of time services and apps are available/operational, and the rate of production incidents (outages, change failures, time-to-recovery): the system's track record of staying up and recovering quickly. An improved rate reduces time teams spend addressing bugs and outages, increasing operational efficiency.

    Other companies applying the metric: DoordashGoodRxMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄EEBO Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Test Coverage

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    The percentage of code covered by automated tests. Shows confidence in change safety.

    Other companies applying the metric: Glassdoor
