12 Developer Experience Metrics in the APEX framework

The APEX framework is referenced by 12 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice. See the APEX source.

  • AI-Written Code Share

    Category: Output📈 Organizational Effectiveness📊 AI Utilization

    Percentage of code drafted or submitted by AI

    Other companies applying the metric: Google
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2025)

  • Capacity accuracy

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The ratio of all completed work (planned plus unplanned) to the work planned for an iteration, measured in story points or issue count.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄LinearB

  • Change Failure Rate

    Category: Quality🏆 Top 5% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    This metric measures the percentage of deployments that cause failures in production, requiring hotfixes or rollbacks. A lower change failure rate indicates a more reliable delivery process.

    Other companies applying the metric: AdyenAmplitudeBooking.comCircleCIDropboxEtsyGitHubGlassdoorLatticeMicrosoftMonzoPelotonToastWebflow

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

  • Number of Defects

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures defect density: the number of errors or failures normalized by a size unit (e.g., function points), sometimes severity-weighted, indicating codebase quality independent of project size.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Unluturk and Kurtel (2016)📄Vogelezang and Heeringen (2019)

  • Pickup time

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The elapsed time between a pull request's creation (or move from draft to ready-for-review) and the start of the review process, i.e., the first non-author review action.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄LinearB

  • Planning accuracy

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    The ratio of planned work actually completed within a sprint or iteration, measured in story points or issue count. High values indicate reliable delivery against commitments; low values indicate scope creep or unplanned work crowding out planned scope.

    Research recommending the metric: 📄LinearB

  • Pull Request Size

    Category: Quality📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    An indicator of overall code review efficiency. Small PRs mean faster pickup time, reviews, and cycles–translating to faster time to value. Larger PRs often result in fewer meaningful comments. For example, PRs with more than 250 lines of code could be tagged.

    Other companies applying the metric: Lloyds Banking GroupMetaMicrosoftPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton

  • Time Spent in Code Review

    Measures the time developers spend reviewing others’ code.
