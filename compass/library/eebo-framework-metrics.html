11 Developer Experience Metrics in the EEBO Framework

The EEBO Framework is referenced by 11 developer experience metrics in the DevEx Metrics Compass, a curated catalogue of DevEx metrics from academic research and industry practice. See the EEBO source.

  • Change Failure Rate

    Category: Quality🏆 Top 5% most-referenced⭐ Product Excellence🎯 AI Impact

    This metric measures the percentage of deployments that cause failures in production, requiring hotfixes or rollbacks. A lower change failure rate indicates a more reliable delivery process.

  • Change Lead Time

    Category: Process🏅 Top 10% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    This metric measures the time it takes for a code commit or change to be successfully deployed to production. It reflects the efficiency of your software delivery process to deliver value to customers.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonAtlassianeBayGlassdoorPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DORA Framework📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Cost Savings

    Category: Business📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Relates to strategic reductions in product development and operational expenses achieved through optimized engineering practices, aligning technical efficiency with business profitability. This is measured by tracking how engineering improvements directly lower costs while maintaining quality and delivery speed.

  • Customer Satisfaction Score

    Category: Customer⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures how pleased customers are with a product, service, or interaction, typically via surveys. Responses are aggregated into a percentage score representing the proportion of satisfied customers

    Research recommending the metric: 📄gun.io (2025)📄McKinsey Framework

  • Deployment Frequency

    Category: Customer🏆 Top 5% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    How often an organization deploys code/builds to a production environment, regardless of whether that change is yet visible to end users. Higher deployment frequency indicates a more agile and responsive delivery process.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonGitLabGlassdoorGoogleMonzoNetflixPelotonSpotifySpotify
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DORA Framework📄DX (2024b)📄DX Core 4 Framework📄Gitpod (2025)📄Hatica (2024)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Orosz and Noda (2024)📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Solanke (2024)

  • Error Rates

    Category: Quality⭐ Product Excellence

    Quantifies the frequency of user or system mistakes during interactions with a product (e.g., failed API requests, UI slips, or task errors)

    Research recommending the metric: 📄Duncan (1988)📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)

  • Mean Time to Recover

    Category: System🏅 Top 10% most-referenced📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Mean Time to Recover (MTTR) is a key DevOps and IT reliability metric that measures the average time it takes to restore a system after a failure. It reflects how quickly teams can diagnose, fix, and recover from incidents. Time from bug report to fix deployment. Shows quality response capability.

    Other companies applying the metric: AdobeDropboxeBayGlassdoorLinkedInMozillaPeloton
    Research recommending the metric: 📄DX (2024b)📄Gitpod (2025)📄Hatica (2024)📄Jetbrains (2024)📄McKinsey Framework📄Peloton📄Sanwal and Deva (2024)📄Srinivasan et al. (2025)

  • Response Time

    Category: Business⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the server’s speed in fulfilling user requests.

  • Revenue Growth Rate

    Category: Business📈 Organizational Effectiveness

    Measures the growth of revenue, such as year over year.

  • Service Uptime Percentage

    Category: System⭐ Product Excellence

    Measures the percentage of time services and apps are available/operational, and the rate of production incidents (outages, change failures, time-to-recovery): the system's track record of staying up and recovering quickly. An improved rate reduces time teams spend addressing bugs and outages, increasing operational efficiency.

    Other companies applying the metric: AmazonDoordashGoodRxMicrosoft
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Gitpod (2025)📄Orosz and Noda (2024)

  • Time to Market for New Features

    Category: Process📈 Organizational Effectiveness🎯 AI Impact

    Measures the duration from the initial conception of a feature to its availability for end-users.

    Other companies applying the metric: MicrosoftUberUber
    Research recommending the metric: 📄Ko (2019)
